Sons difficiles / Sonidos difíciles / Difficult sounds

🔸 [ʁ] — R français, grasseyé au fond de la gorge (ex : "brûle", "frères").

🔸 [ɑ̃] / [ɔ̃] — voyelles nasales (ex : "cendres", "encore").

🔸 [ɾ] vs [r] — vibrante simple ("pero") vs double ("corrieron").

🔸 [x] — jota gutturale (ex : "hijos", "argelia").

🔸 [θ] / [ð] — "th" sourd et sonore (ex : "breath", "the").

🔸 [ŋ] — "ng" nasal final (ex : "running", "burning").

🌍 Foreign words in the bridge: "Agua" (Spanish), "Fire" (English), "Fogo" (Portuguese) — overlapping shouts from firefighters worldwide.
